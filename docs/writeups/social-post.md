# Social post draft (Social Blitz Prize)

Rules for this prize: post on any social platform during the submission period. The post must **begin** with `#KiroweenHackathon26`. It must share your favourite thing about Kiro and how it changed the way you approach development. Judges look at the quality of the post and its insight about Kiro. Add the post's link to the Devpost submission.

The Kiro lines are marked **FILL IN**. They have to be your real experience, so write them after you have built with Kiro.
