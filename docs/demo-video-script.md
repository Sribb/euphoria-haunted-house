# Demo video script

Target length: **2:50**. Judges do not have to watch past 3:00. Record the game at 1280x720 or higher with game sound on. Upload to YouTube as Public.

Rules that apply: show the project working on the device it was built for (a laptop browser, plus a few seconds on a phone), no copyrighted music (the game's own synthesised score is fine), no third-party trademarks.

Read the voiceover out loud once before recording and change any line that does not sound like you. About 380 spoken words.

## Shot list to record

1. Fresh load, title, intro card, first steps in the Foyer (30 s).
2. The drawer clue, start to candle (45 s raw, cut to about 25).
3. The Miser encounter with one wrong answer first (60 s raw, cut to about 30).
4. The freed moment and the walk through the door into the Cellar (30 s).
5. The Cellar: the barrels, one movie beat, the Gambler (20 s).
6. Kiro: your real screen (60 s raw, cut to 35).
7. A phone in hand playing it (10 s).

## Tips

- Clear saved progress before recording: open the game, then run `localStorage.clear()` in the browser console and reload.
- Each movie runs about 30 seconds and cannot be skipped. Record them whole and cut in the edit.
- Keep the game's sound under your voice. The music box starts when a movie plays.
