How to play

Welcome to the
haunted house

This house is Euphoria's investing course, haunted. Every room is one short lesson, and every ghost is stuck here until someone understands it.

  1. Explore with your lantern. Glowing wisps mark the clues.
  2. Look at each clue. It plays a short movie and lights one of the room's three candles.
  3. Face the ghost. With all three candles lit, answer its questions to set it free and open the next door.
